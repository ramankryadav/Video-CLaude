# The documentary formula ("The ENTIRE History of X in N Minutes")

This formula is used for The Long Story channel (@thelongstoryyy). It was proven on the Panama Canal and Taiwan scripts and modelled on the GoDocs video "How a 3,000-Year-Old Obsession Built the Suez Canal" (1.57M views, 11 min).

## What the reference measured
- **Pace ~161 wpm** in GoDocs. Our Zeno VO runs ~150–154 wpm, so budget words with 152.
- **A 50-second hook montage** of every failed attempt, then "It wasn't X… it was Y", then "Now, let's take a closer look at…".
- **Chronological mini-arcs:** each attempt or ruler goes ambition → suffering → ironic failure (a superstition, a sandstorm, a survey error).
- **Vivid, simple, slightly dramatic wording:** "clanking iron monsters", "laughed out loud", "Enter Ferdinand de Lesseps". Third person throughout, except the "you" in the opening line.
- **The ending** is a short modern status, then a question for the comments.

## Structure (15 min ≈ 2,300 words ≈ 50 boxes)

| Part | Boxes | What happens | Example lines |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cold open** | 4 | The user's fixed opener, "**This is X. You probably think …**", then a montage of every claimant or failure in 3–6 short punches, a stakes line, then "It wasn't A, and it wasn't B. **It was C.** Now, let's take a closer look at the story of …" | Panama: "Spanish kings looked at it… Scotland lost a huge share of the money… France… watched him go bankrupt." Taiwan: "The Dutch… lost it to the son of a pirate. A Chinese emperor… called it a ball of mud." |
| **The geography / the problem** | 2–3 | "Look at a map of …": shape, size and why the place matters. A good "think about that" awe line | "Two giant continents, joined by one skinny strip of land…" |
| **Attempt / era 1…N** | 3–6 each | Each era is one mini-arc: **Enter [person]** with a look or backstory, the plan, the suffering (numbers, disease, war), then the **ironic twist**. Use real names and dates | Koxinga took the fort, "and then, the twist… died at thirty-seven". De Lesseps did "exactly what he did at Suez". |
| **The human cost** | 1–2 | A real death toll or suffering, stated plainly with "an estimated" | "an estimated twenty-two thousand people died" |
| **Turning point** | 2–4 | The person or idea that finally worked, framed as "The man who saved it wasn't…" | Gorgas and the mosquitoes; "The enemy became the engine." |
| **The build / the modern era** | 4–8 | How it worked, then milestones. One **"Remember that number, it comes back later"** plant | "fifty million gallons… comes back to haunt the canal later" |
| **Today's stakes** | 3–5 | Present-day status, a callback to the planted number, the neutral current debate | The 2023 drought; "an American president said…" |
| **Recap + question** | 2 | "So that's X. Not A, but B, C, D…" (callbacks), a last image, then "**So what do you think will happen to X in the next fifty years?** Tell me in the comments, and subscribe for the next long story." | |

## Devices that make it work
- **The opening line is fixed:** "This is [X]. You probably think [common belief]." The user asked for this exact opener.
- **Ironic twist per era:** every chapter ends on a reversal ("Fifty years of rule ended in a single afternoon").
- **Callbacks:** plant 1–2 numbers or objects early and pay them off in the "today" part (Panama: 50 million gallons → the drought).
- **"Enter [name]":** use it for the big characters.
- **Strangeness lines:** "Think about how strange this is.", "So one of the strangest facts in modern history is this." Use them once or twice per video.
- **Section headings** are short with years: `## The French gamble (1881)`. They become chapter markers later.
- **Section starts** are `[natural pace]` plus a tone tag.

## Tone and safety
- Neutral on current politics: say what each side claims, never predict a war, no gore. Battles are told at map level.
- Hedge uncertain anecdotes ("reportedly", "according to a famous story", "supposedly").
- Real people are fine in the script. In image prompts they're described by look (that's the image skill's rule).

- Title: "The ENTIRE History of [X] in [N] Minutes". Screen the exact title first with `entire-history-studio/scripts/publish/yt_research.py comps`.
