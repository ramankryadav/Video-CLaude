## Tips
- AI generators often garble words. If the text comes out wrong, delete the text sentence from the prompt, generate the image clean, then add the text in Canva or Photoshop (heavy sans-serif such as Anton or Bebas Neue, yellow or white fill, thick black stroke).
- Upload your Panama or Taiwan thumbnail as a style or character reference when the tool allows it, so the mascot matches across videos.
- Leave the bottom-right corner free of important detail. YouTube's timestamp covers it.
- Check every result at phone size (about 168px wide) before choosing.
- Run #1 vs #2 vs #3 in YouTube's "Test & Compare".
